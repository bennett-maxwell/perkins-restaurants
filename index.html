Perkins LLC franchises full-service family restaurants operating as Perkins Restaurant and Bakery, a brand serving all-day breakfast, lunch, dinner, and bakery since 1958. Its Franchise Disclosure Document reports 257 restaurants open at the end of fiscal 2024, including 175 franchised and 82 company-owned locations. Item 19 shows 168 reporting franchised restaurants averaging $1,933,346 in annual sales with a $1,929,925 median. The standard franchise fee is $40,000 for a 20-year term, and the estimated initial investment for a traditional restaurant ranges from $1,443,890 to $3,581,375. Franchisees pay a 4% weekly royalty plus a 3% brand fund contribution and 1% local marketing requirement, and territories are non-exclusive.
